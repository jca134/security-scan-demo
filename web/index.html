How are my files kept private?

Everything is checked locally, in this browser tab. Privacy Scanner has no server, so your files are never uploaded or sent to us or anyone else.

  • Your browser talks to Google Drive directly, using the access you approve when you sign in.
  • Sharing only reads who has access to each file. File contents are never opened.
  • Sharing + content reads a sample (the first 500 words unless you choose more) from files that are already shared with others and checks it with a model that runs locally on your device.
  • Already private and restricted files are automatically skipped and never read.
  • Nothing is saved. Close the tab and the results are gone.

How to fix this
  1. Update to the latest version of Chrome or Edge, which support the in-browser checker. Open this page over https:// or on localhost.
  2. Turn on hardware acceleration. In Chrome, go to Settings → System and turn on Use graphics acceleration when available, then click Relaunch. In Edge, it's under Settings → System and performance.
  3. Check your GPU status: paste chrome://gpu (or edge://gpu) into a new tab and look for WebGPU: Hardware accelerated. If it says disabled or software only, your graphics card or driver may be blocked by the browser.
  4. Update your browser and graphics drivers, then restart your computer.
  5. Remote desktop sessions, virtual machines, and some battery-saver modes hide the GPU from the browser. Try again directly on your computer and plugged in.
  6. Still stuck? Switch to Sharing only above, which doesn't need a GPU, or try another computer.

Checking more of each file can catch sensitive details further down, but takes longer. Files over 20 MB are always skipped.
